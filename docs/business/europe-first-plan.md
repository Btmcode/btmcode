# BTMCode — Europe-first commercial plan

## Positioning
Independent software developer delivering custom web applications, SaaS platforms, AI integrations and workflow automation. Direct founder/developer collaboration; no agency hand-off.

## Target markets
Start with English-language B2B buyers across the EU/EEA and UK. Prioritise Germany, Netherlands, Ireland, Nordics and English-speaking technology teams; localise landing pages and case studies only when there is a credible offer and proof. Do not claim local offices or local certifications.

## Revenue model
1. Fixed-scope project work: paid discovery, milestone-based delivery, written acceptance criteria and change control.
2. Monthly Care: recurring maintenance, dependency/security updates, backups and a defined monthly support allowance. State exclusions and response windows; do not promise 24/7 monitoring unless implemented.
3. Growth & Automation: monthly SEO/analytics review, conversion improvements and workflow automation.
4. Micro-SaaS: build one narrow product from repeated client problems; monthly/annual subscriptions after support, billing, tax and cancellation flows are ready.

"Passive income" is not fully passive: SaaS needs maintenance, support, compliance, billing and churn management. Treat it as recurring product revenue, not guaranteed passive profit.

## Initial offer architecture (indicative, not published pricing)
- Discovery Sprint: paid, fixed deliverable and decision report.
- Build Sprint: milestone-based custom implementation.
- Care Plan: monthly retainer with capped hours and response SLA.
- Automation Retainer: monthly improvement backlog and usage limits.

Quote in EUR for EU clients; quote scope, assumptions, deliverables, acceptance, revisions, support window, IP transfer/licensing, third-party costs, payment milestones, late payment, cancellation and governing law in a written agreement. Review cross-border tax, VAT and invoicing with a Turkish accountant before quoting final tax-inclusive prices.

## Lead funnel
English landing page -> relevant case study -> concise project brief form -> discovery call (WhatsApp/email/Meet) -> paid discovery or proposal -> milestone delivery -> monthly Care offer.

## Trust proof
Use factual signals only: HTTPS connection, independent developer, direct communication, written scope before proposal, real project links and accurately described security practices. Never display fake ISO/SOC/GDPR/SSL certification seals, fake reviews, client logos or unverified metrics.

## Europe legal checklist
- Assess GDPR territorial scope under Article 3(2) when offering services to people in the EU.
- Assess Article 27 EU representative requirement and its exceptions with counsel.
- Review ePrivacy/local cookie and direct-marketing rules per target country.
- Review KVKK Article 9 transfer mechanism for Vercel, Google and Resend.
- Confirm Turkish tax status, export-of-services treatment, VAT place-of-supply and invoice requirements with an accountant.
- Use B2B service agreements and distinguish business customers from consumers.
