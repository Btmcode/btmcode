# Cross-border freelancer operations — Turkey to Europe

**Working note, 7 November 2026. Not tax or legal advice. Confirm with a Turkish SMMM/YMM and EU tax adviser before invoicing.**

## 1. Turkish freelancer status
The project owner describes the activity as freelance software development but has not registered a company or sole proprietorship. The GİB's guidance on commencement of activity identifies several indicators for self-employed professionals, including opening a professional workplace, displaying professional signage, continuously advertising professional activity, or registering with a professional body. Taxable self-employed professionals must notify commencement; GİB guidance also describes e-SMM obligations. The exact commencement date and classification depend on facts and should be reviewed by an accountant before paid recurring work is accepted.

Official source: https://gib.gov.tr/vergi-konulari/2_isletme_ve_girisimci/13_mukelleflerin_ise_baslama_ve_isi_birakma_odevleri/13

## 2. Turkish VAT and service export
Under KDV Law 3065 Articles 11/1-a and 12/2, a service may qualify for service-export VAT exemption when:
- it is performed for a customer abroad; and
- the benefit/use of the service is abroad, connected with the customer's foreign business, transactions or activities and not its activities in Türkiye.

A foreign customer address alone is not enough. The contract, invoice, actual beneficiary/use, and supporting records matter. Obtain written accountant advice for each service type, especially custom software, consulting, support retainers and SaaS subscriptions.

Official source: https://cdn.gib.gov.tr/api/gibportal-file/file/getFileResources?objectKey=arsiv%2Ffileadmin%2Fuser_upload%2FTebligler%2FKDV%2Fkdv_genteb.htm

## 3. EU VAT treatment
Do not treat every software-related sale identically:
- B2B services generally follow the customer's establishment as place of supply under VAT Directive Article 44; Article 196 may make the business customer liable under reverse charge, subject to the applicable rules and evidence.
- B2C services generally follow the supplier's establishment under Article 45, but electronically supplied services have special rules (including Article 58) and may be taxed where the consumer resides.
- A custom software project, maintenance service, downloadable digital product and SaaS subscription may need different classification.

Official sources:
- https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-directive/place-taxation_en
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32006L0112

## 4. GDPR and marketing
If the business intentionally offers services to people in the EU or monitors behaviour of people in the EU, GDPR territorial scope under Article 3(2) must be assessed. Article 27 may require a written EU representative unless a narrow exception applies. EDPB Guidelines 3/2018 explain that incidental access by an EU resident is not enough by itself; intentional targeting is relevant.

Email marketing and cookie rules must also be reviewed against ePrivacy requirements and the national implementation in each target country. Turkish IYS compliance does not automatically satisfy EU rules.

Official sources:
- https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-32018-territorial-scope-gdpr-article-3-version_en
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679

## 5. Before first EU invoice
- Confirm tax registration and activity code with SMMM.
- Confirm invoice/e-SMM document type and currency handling.
- Confirm VAT export exemption eligibility per service and customer use.
- Collect business customer legal name, address and VAT ID where relevant; validate customer status.
- Use written B2B agreement with scope, milestones, payment terms, acceptance, change requests, IP/licensing, confidentiality, data processing and termination.
- Keep contract, invoice, delivery evidence, customer correspondence and foreign-payment records.
- Do not promise tax-free service or reverse charge without professional confirmation.
